AMG Girls' Hostel Operations Manual

Simple step-by-step guide to operating the software

Welcome to the AMG Girls' Hostel Management System guide. Below is the step-by-step operations manual to help warden, clerk, and administrative users operate the system effectively.

tune 1. Initial Setup: Rooms & Fee Configuration

Configure room categories, structures, and payment rules before admitting students. Setting these up properly ensures that the system generates correct bills automatically.

  1. Room Types: Go to Fee Setup > Room Types tab. Create categories like "Premium Triple", "Classic Double", or "AC Single".
  2. Fee Structures: Go to Fee Setup > Fee Structures tab. Create a fee structure (e.g. 2026-27 Double Room) and tie it to a Room Type.
  3. Fee Heads & Priorities (Crucial Step): Under Fee Setup > Fee Heads, select the structure and add individual charges:
    • Security Deposit (Refundable): Select Frequency as One-time (on joining), and set Payment Priority to 1 (highest).
    • Accommodation Charges: Select Frequency as Bi-annually, and set Payment Priority to 2.
    • Mess Charges: Select Frequency as Bi-annually, and set Payment Priority to 3.
info
Important Priority Flow: Setting Security Deposit (Priority 1), Accommodation (Priority 2), and Mess (Priority 3) ensures that when making receipts, students pay Semester 1 fees sequentially before being allowed to pay Semester 2 fees.

meeting_room 2. Adding Hostel Rooms

  1. Go to Settings and scroll to the Add Hostel Room card.
  2. Enter the Room Number (e.g. 101), select the Floor, select the Room Type, and input the Capacity / Bed count (e.g. 3 beds).
  3. Click Create Room. The room will now appear in your active inventory and be available for allotments.

assignment_ind 3. Admissions & Applications

  1. Public Student Admission Link: Share the public form link (available by logging out and clicking the Student Registration link). Students fill out their details online.
  2. Reviewing Applications: In the admin panel, click Applications. Locate new students in the Pending tab.
  3. Click Review to verify their details, write optional internal remarks, and click Approve or Reject.

bed 4. Room Allotment & Vacating

  1. Go to Room Allotment in the sidebar. Approved students are listed under Approved (Pending Allotment).
  2. Click Allot Room, select an available room from the list, set their expected vacating date, and confirm.
  3. Automatic Ledger: The moment you click confirm, the system automatically writes all deposit, accommodation, and monthly/bi-annual bills to the student's personal fee ledger.
  4. Vacating: Under Active Allotments, click Vacate Room to mark a student as departed and clear the bed in inventory.

payments 5. Collecting Fees & Recording Receipts

  1. Go to Fee Receipts > Pending Fees. Use the search bar to locate the student.
  2. Select the check-boxes of the bills they want to pay.
  3. Click the green Collect Selected button.
  4. In the popup modal, enter the payment mode (UPI, Cash, Bank Transfer), actual paid amount, transaction ID (if any), and click Record Payment.
  5. Printing Invoices: Go to Fee Receipts > All Receipts, find the payment record, and click Print Receipt to download/print a professional receipt invoice.

bar_chart 6. Printing Reports

  • Occupancy: Go to Reports > Occupancy to see total beds filled, empty rooms, and detailed room-by-room charts.
  • Fee Collection: Go to Reports > Fee Collection to verify income logs filtered by month or academic year.
  • Student Register: Go to Reports > Student Register to get a clean printable register containing student contact numbers, courses, emergency details, and room numbers.

settings 7. Customizing Settings

  • Organization Details: Under Settings, edit the organization name, tagline, address, contact, and upload your custom branding logo to print on all student receipts.
  • Staff Accounts: In Settings > User Accounts, click Add User to create logins for wardens, helpers, or system administrators.
  • Academic Session Rollover: At the end of the year, input the new year name (e.g., `2027-28`) and trigger the rollover. All active residents are carried forward and outstanding balances become opening balances for the new session.
